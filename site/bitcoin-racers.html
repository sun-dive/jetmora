A race costs about ninety entries. Nobody pays to play; the operator refuels.

Every tick above was fetched from the log and checked against its root with an RFC 6962 inclusion proof, computed here rather than taken on trust. The chain has no blocks, no coin and no miners. Its ordering and structure are proved; its timestamps are its own claim.
