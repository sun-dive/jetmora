# Contributing

Contributions are welcome, and one term keeps the project free to evolve.

**By contributing, you license your contribution to sun-dive under the Apache License 2.0**, including
its patent grant (§3). That lets the contribution be included in the repository under the Business Source
License 1.1 now, and converted with everything else to Apache 2.0 on the Change Date, without anyone
having to be asked again. It also means no contribution can later be used to restrict the project or its
author.

Nothing else is required. Keep the file header, keep the NOTICE, and read `spec/log.md` before changing
anything the specification governs.
